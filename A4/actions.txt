# Format: action_name|description|valid_targets (comma-separated)
take|Pick up an item or object.|items
give|Release an item to a character.|items, characters
drop|Release an item from your inventory.|items
inspect|Inspect an item or location.|locations, items
talk to|Speak with a character.|characters
eat|Eat an item.|items
drink|Drink an item.|items
enter|Enter a door or opening.|exits
go|Move in a direction.|directions
follow|Follow a character.|characters
